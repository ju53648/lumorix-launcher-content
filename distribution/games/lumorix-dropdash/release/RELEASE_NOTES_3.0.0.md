# VENSUKO DropDash 3.0.0

**BUILD YOUR COMBO. BEAT YOUR LIMITS.**

Version 3.0.0 is DropDash's biggest content update yet, introducing a fully redesigned audio experience, an extensive achievement system, and a refreshed VENSUKO identity.

## New: 102 Achievements

Unlock 102 achievements across core collection, scoring, streaks, shielding, power-ups, ranked modes and special challenges. Progress is saved locally between sessions. Four difficulty tiers — Standard, Advanced, Expert and Legendary — and new filters help you track what to tackle next. High-end goals include a 50-core streak, 100,000 points in one run, and beating Classic on Hard without collecting a core.

**Note:** Achievement progress is saved on the current device; synchronization with the VENSUKO account is not part of this release.

## New: Sound System 2.0

A fully original sound pack with 20 new effects and two electronic music loops. Core pickups and combos, shield pulses and blocks, power-ups, crashes, wave transitions, and achievements now have distinct audio feedback. New music moves smoothly between menu and gameplay and increases intensity in Rush 30 and Hard.

Open **Sound & Music** from the main menu or game over, or press **M** in a run. Adjust master, music and sound-effect volumes independently, mute everything, and test a sound. Your settings are saved on the device.

## Improved gameplay and presentation

- New VENSUKO DropDash banner, cover, icon and updated game branding.
- Shield deflections award bonus points and milestone rewards.
- The achievement menu pauses active gameplay and resumes where you left off.
- Improved display and keyboard controls, including responsive achievement filters and sound settings.
- Existing game modes and local/worldwide leaderboards are preserved, including launcher-assisted score submission.

## Availability

- Windows desktop build: 3.0.0.
- macOS builds: Apple Silicon (arm64) and Intel (x64) when both release packages pass verification.
- The VENSUKO Launcher will offer the update when the public game manifest is promoted.
