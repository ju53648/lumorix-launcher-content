# VENSUKO DropDash 3.1.0

- Choose your own look from ten distinct character designs.
- Worldwide leaderboards now show public player avatars and update automatically.
- The leaderboard moves to a dedicated sidebar and makes room for gameplay when a run starts.
- New five-second countdown before each run: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, START.
- The playfield expands smoothly in the first two seconds; no gameplay time or score is counted before START.
- Sound and music controls are now grouped under Settings.
- Existing achievements, rankings, saves, modes and audio remain supported.
